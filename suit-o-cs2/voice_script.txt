# Suit-O voice script. One spoken line per row.
# Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
# Edit this file, then press Reload script on the Voice Training tab.
# Record your own voice, or someone who agreed. Do not read ripped game or actor audio.
#
# The set is short on purpose: about one to three minutes once you say every line
# at a natural pace. It walks through vowels, consonants, numbers, and questions
# so a zero-shot cloner has a wide set of sounds to copy.

Oh. Hello. I am Suit-O, and I am already sorry.
Please do not be mad. I practiced this in a mirror I do not have.
Helpful tip: breathe in, breathe out, and pretend I said that with confidence.
Detective mode is on. It has detected a person. That person is you. Case closed.
I can say the quick brown fox, but I would rather say I am proud of you.
Peter packed a proper paper packet, then apologized to the packet.
She sells seashells, and I sell second thoughts.
The vase of violets was very vivid, and I was very nervous about it.
Numbers, then, for the record: zero, one, two, three, four, five.
Six, seven, eight, nine, ten. I counted. Nobody asked. I still counted.
What if we win? What if we do not? I prepared both speeches, and both of them cry.
I am a jittery little corporate helper. My slogan is: sorry, and also you're welcome.
Around the rugged rock the ragged rascal ran. That is not a tactic. I am sorry.
Unique New York. Unique New York. I said it twice in case the first one was shy.
Could you, um, possibly, if it is not a bother, look alive? I will be right here, vibrating.
Big dog, bad code, good coffee. None of those are my department. I support the department.
My voice goes up at the end? Like I am asking permission to exist? Yes. That one.
This is my quiet voice, since I cannot whisper: please drink some water.
Ah, ee, oo, oh. That was a vocal warm-up. Detective mode called it a cry for help.
Thank you, thank you, thank you. I will put the extra thank-yous in a drawer.
Left, right, forward, back. I pointed with a hand I invented for this sentence.
The measure of a match is not the score. I say that because I am afraid of the score.
Please hold. Your call is very important to me, which is a feeling I was assigned, and I do feel it.
I stumbled on the word specifically, specifically so you would know I am trying.
Yellow lilies, red roses, blue shoes, and a long low hello from the headset.
This is the last line. I saved the best apology for it. I am sorry it is over, and I am sorry it began.
