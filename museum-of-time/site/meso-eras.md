# A Day in Meso: eras of Mesoamerica (reference; source: Wikipedia, "Mesoamerican chronology")
Dates are approximate and vary by region. Real history; tag every beat documented / reconstructed / invented.

- Paleo-Indian: to ~3500 BCE (wikipedia table), Archaic ~8000-2600 BCE: first villages, maize farming
- Preclassic / Formative: 2000 BCE-250 CE. Olmec (La Venta, San Lorenzo), early Maya (Nakbe, El Mirador), Zapotec (Monte Alban), Cuicuilco
- Classic: 250-900 CE. Teotihuacan (to ~600-750), Monte Alban, Maya city-states (Tikal, Calakmul, Copan, Palenque)
- Early Postclassic: 900-1200. Tula (Toltec), Mitla, Chichen Itza
- Late Postclassic: 1200-1521. Tenochtitlan (founded 1325), Triple Alliance (1430), Mayapan, K'iche' Q'umarkaj, Iximche
- Colonial: 1521-1821. Spanish conquest, friars, encomienda (Mani book burning 1562 falls here)
- Postcolonial: 1821-present
