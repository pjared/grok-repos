# Museum of Time — long-term site rules (locked 2026-10-05)

## Halls
### Wheel of Travelers
**Door:** Parable. Invented exhibits about people who changed a timeline.  
**Look past entrance:** clean museum light  
**Plaque voice:** parable / fiction cue (Iole Scene 0 template: not a history)

### Surviving Books
**Door:** Sourced retelling. Myth and history kept alive after the fires.  
**Look past entrance:** firelight and paper texture  
**Plaque voice:** sourced retelling — never the travelers register

## Shared entrance
Every exhibit opens with the same shot: camera dollies down a dark wing toward one lit pedestal (Iole F1). On the website, that shot is the walk into each room. Halls only differ after that point.

## Hard rules
1. Epistemic status on the door — never optional.
2. Pedestal plaque voices do not bleed across wings.
3. Fiction mechanics (failed provenance, un-archivable feeling) stay story-logic; never presented as real science or art history.
- Rule: "What people believed" room holds conspiracies framed as beliefs, beside documented plaques. Real crimes/victims (e.g., Epstein, atrocities) stay on fixed sourced plaques, never in time war fiction or on the ballot; center survivors, no named associates without court findings.
- Names rule (team consensus, the director delegated): real names only on documented plaques, for people convicted or central to the public record; survivors named only if they spoke publicly under their own name; fiction halls use invented names only.
