# A Day in Meso: story outline (working, 2026-10-10)
Register: dramatized history. Real events, invented household. Every beat tagged documented / reconstructed / invented. Voice: Witness 1. Content note on the door for the colonial days.

## Series spine
- Invented scribe records a high priest's words as holy text. Priest is heard only in fragments the scribe murmurs; no invented scripture spoken in full.
- Same three objects open every day: pot, maize, codex. Codices: scribe's book (tall, dark cover), market ledger (short, wide, scuffed; invented non-numeral tally marks), family almanac (small, cord-tied). Stills in assets/stills/codex/.
- Real rulers stay off camera; the king in the side hustle is an invented lord, unnamed.

## Day 1: Triple Alliance, Late Postclassic (c. 1430-1519)
Setting: Tlacopan (junior partner of the alliance; documented-ish: smaller tribute share, Scholar to confirm).
- Dawn: household kitchen, pot, maize. (reconstructed)
- Midday: public market; market officials oversee trade (documented, Scholar to confirm). Side hustle: a sealed jar/bundle moves from a stall to a back room; scribe notices and says nothing. Lord seen only as litter/retinue. (invented)
- Afternoon: scribe's workroom, priest's fragments. (invented)
- Dusk: family meal, almanac; calm sky, audience knows what's coming. (invented)

## Later days
- Spanish arrival: routine goes slightly wrong; ships, unfamiliar sounds; no battle.
- Colonial day: reverent, slow, off-screen violence, ends on the warm pot with nobody at it. Scribe carries the book through.
- Maní 1562: parked.
