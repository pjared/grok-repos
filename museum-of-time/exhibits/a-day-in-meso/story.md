# A Day in Meso: the story so far (story only, from the director's pitches)
Slice of life, inspired by the Triple Alliance years. People, places and events are invented.

1. A war begins over a refused tribute bundle, but the real cause is private: a general with a house near the market (low door, high stair) and shady alliances, backed by a king. Red banners mark this side.
2. A scribe in Tlacopan writes down a high priest's words as holy text. The priest's war speech, written down by the scribe, blames someone else entirely.
3. At the market, a steward's crooked scale and a moving jar trace back to the general. A woman from the house sees everything.
4. A rival kingdom in green, with its own cast, watches the war grow. Across the Atlantic, another court plays the same game.
5. The years run toward the Spanish arrival. The scribe's silence is what he carries into that day.
Later: Maní day; Nahuatl-teaching museum app.
