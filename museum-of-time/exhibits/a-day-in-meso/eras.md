# A Day in Meso: Mesoamerican eras (pinned reference)
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerican_chronology and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerica
Dates are approximate and vary by region and by author. Verify against the pages before any plaque; Scholar signs off.

- Archaic: c. 8000-2000 BCE
- Preclassic (Formative): c. 2000 BCE-250 CE (Early, Middle, Late)
- Classic: c. 250-900 CE (Early, Late, Terminal)
- Postclassic: c. 900-1521 CE (Early, Late)
- Spanish conquest and colonial era: 1519 onward (Mani book burning: 1562)

Series rules: one era and place per day; tag each beat documented / reconstructed / invented; same pot, maize, codex open every day.
