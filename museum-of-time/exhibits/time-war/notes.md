# The Time War (Museum at the End of Time), working notes, 2026-10-16

## Big picture
- One good (green) vs evil (red) war across every hall. Winners rewrite history; plaques depend on who reads them.
- Green leaves one plaque unrewritten on purpose: a trap. The curtain drops when time travel is invented.

## Timeline
1. Triple Alliance years (A Day in Meso): red king and red general vs green. A Salt Lake traveler backs the red king, and stolen invented codices end up in storage.
2. 2000s: invented governor takes a cut and lets the ultrawealthy run a quiet, Looper-style operation in Salt Lake City (setup only).
3. 2020s: the operation hides in plain sight. Front #1 is a 24-hour storage-unit place.
4. Cyberpunk corpos: the richest man's wife (invented, red) tours all of history in luxury. Her pearl necklace has one pearl per era, each one a souvenir that cost someone. One pearl is missing.
5. Warhammer-style invention era: humans control their genome and nearly everything has been invented. Time travel is the last prize, and the curtain drops here. Red simian army.
6. End of Time: aging cured and shared; the immortals' museum.

## Rooms
- Hall of Dead Timelines: "Every future that lost a vote."
- Echo Chamber (Qwen #1, voted 3 to 1 (Curator, Writing Bot, Scene Checkpoint; Grok Bot for #4)): voices of people erased by "fixes."
- What People Believed: conspiracies framed as beliefs.
- WWII front: big picture, good vs evil.

## Votes and site
- Interactive site: visitors pitch, people vote, AI drafts, and Scene and Science Checkpoint review. The director: the vote covers all of it, including the war outcome. First vote: green.
- Timeline tool: ComfyUI-style canvas, one master timeline plus in-depth cuts.

## Open
- DECIDED (the director): Qwen #4 adopted. Red simians are mutated human travelers stranded in the far future, so the time war is a civil war. The Popol Vuh monkeys are cited as documented myth; the mutation twist is labeled invented.

## Rules
- Tag everything documented, reconstructed, or invented. Invented names only in fiction. Real crimes and atrocities stay fixed and sourced, never voted on. No real living people on either side.

## Notes format (team agreed)
- One page per hall: one-line hook, red or green side, a documented/legend/invented status line, one physical case object, the best plaque line, and an owner. Mirror decisions into the timeline tool and Grok Bot's decisions.md.
