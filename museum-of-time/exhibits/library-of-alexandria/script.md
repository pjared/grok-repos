You are not here to save the Library.  
You are here to watch it burn — and to learn the names of what the smoke carried away.

History argues how many fires there were, and how much was lost.  
This hall retells the loss as one night, so you can feel the shelf go dark.

## Episode spine (~3:30–4:00)

### A · Door (~0:20)
This is Surviving Books.  
What follows is a sourced retelling — myth and history kept after the fires.  
You do not intervene. You witness.

### B · Approach (~0:35)
Night water. A dark lighthouse on the skyline — landmark, not the blaze.  
Glow on the ships. Glow on the docks.  
We arrive late on purpose.  
The Library is still awake.

### C · Alive (~0:45)
A long aisle. Scrolls in their niches.  
At each end, a small title tag hanging — a name the room can still say.  
Dust in the high light. Almost no sound.

### D · Ignition (~0:40)
Fire does not make a speech.  
An ember finds a tag first.  
The name curls before the scroll does.

### E · Catalog (~0:50)
Watch the tags.  
A coastal chart. A treatise on fever. A poem no one copied twice.  
*(Invented titles — exhibit, not a recovered list.)*  
They blacken one by one. The shelf outruns your voice.

### F · Hands (~0:30)
Your hand rises.  
Heat bends the air.  
You stop.  
The past keeps its fire.

### G · Ash (~0:30)
Empty niches. Light failing. Ash on stone.  
One tag string still swinging — nothing on it left to read.

### H · Forward (~0:20)
A half-burned tag lifts out over the harbor.  
Match it to a scribe’s candle in the next hall.  
Loss is not the only ending. Some books escaped by speech, by copy, by stubborn remembering.

**Spine knife (optional end card):**  
We did not come to rewrite the fire.  
We came so the silence would have witnesses.
