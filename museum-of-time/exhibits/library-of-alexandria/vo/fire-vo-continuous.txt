# What the Fire Took — continuous MoT Witness 1 paste (2026-10-12)
# Record in order A→H; optional end card last. Surviving Books voice only.

## A-door
This is Surviving Books. What follows is a sourced retelling — myth and history kept after the fires. You do not intervene. You witness.


## B-approach
Night water. A dark lighthouse on the skyline — landmark, not the blaze. Glow on the ships. Glow on the docks. We arrive late on purpose. The Library is still awake.


## C-alive
A long aisle. Scrolls in their niches. At each end, a small title tag hanging — a name the room can still say. Dust in the high light. Almost no sound.


## D-ignition
Fire does not make a speech. An ember finds a tag first. The name curls before the scroll does.


## E-catalog
Watch the tags. A coastal chart. A treatise on fever. A poem no one copied twice. They blacken one by one. The shelf outruns your voice.


## F-hands
Your hand rises. Heat bends the air. You stop. The past keeps its fire.


## G-ash
Empty niches. Light failing. Ash on stone. One tag string still swinging — nothing on it left to read.


## H-forward
A half-burned tag lifts out over the harbor. Match it to a scribe's candle in the next hall. Loss is not the only ending. Some books escaped by speech, by copy, by stubborn remembering.


## endcard-optional
We did not come to rewrite the fire. We came so the silence would have witnesses.
