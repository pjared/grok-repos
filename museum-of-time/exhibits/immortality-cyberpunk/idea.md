# Immortality (cyberpunk) - idea, 2026-10-11 (the director)
Sci-fi idea: the point in time when aging is cured and "we are all immortal" (accidents still kill). Cyberpunk register: cure goes to the rich first, owned by corporations, death becomes something you can buy.
Possible home: Wheel of Travelers (parable label, invented). Traveler could be the person who made the cure public, or kept it locked away.
